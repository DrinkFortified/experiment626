    What's the difference between Energy and Everyday?
    Both share the same electrolyte base. Energy is a higher-sodium formula with natural caffeine from coffee bean. Everyday is caffeine-free and adds Vitamin D3 and Vitamin K2.
    
        
    Can I mix formulas and flavors?
    Yes. Mix any combination of Energy and Everyday in Organic Salty Orange and Organic Salty Watermelon. Rewards are based on the total number of packs in your bundle.
    
        
    How does Subscribe & Save work?
    You save 10% on every order and choose delivery every 30, 60, or 90 days. Your subscription renews automatically at the price shown until you cancel, and we email you a reminder before each renewal.
    
        
    How do I skip, change, or cancel?
    Log in at livefortified.com and go to My Subscriptions. You can skip, swap flavors, change quantity, or cancel anytime before your next billing date. No calls or emails required. You can also email hello@livefortified.com or call (888) 688-8351.
    
        
    What is Free Creatine For Life?
    Keep 3 or more packs on an active subscription and we include our single-ingredient Creatine Monohydrate with your orders at no charge, for as long as your subscription stays at 3+ packs.
    
        
    What if I don't like it?
    Contact us within 30 days of delivery and we'll refund the purchase price. No return required. A refund does not cancel future subscription orders on its own, so cancel from your account if you'd like to stop.
    
        
    When will my order ship?
    Orders typically ship within 1 to 3 business days and standard ground delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days. We ship to all 50 US states and DC.
