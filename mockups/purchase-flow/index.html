    For the team

    Why this flow should convert

    1. Builder above the fold. The hero is compressed so step 1 is visible on load. The original page puts the builder fourth.
    2. Prefilled defaults. 2 packs, a mixed box, and Subscribe & Save every 30 days are selected on load, so a visitor can tap Checkout with zero decisions.
    3. Bundle size first. Choosing quantity before flavor anchors on the bundle (and its rewards), which tends to lift average order value.
    4. Visible reward ladder. The progress bar names the next unlock ("Add 1 more pack for Free Shipping") at every step.
    5. Sticky summary and mobile bar. The total and CTA stay in view on desktop and phone.
    6. Express pay. Shop Pay and Apple Pay are offered beside the main CTA to skip form filling.
    7. One-page checkout. Contact, shipping, and payment in one screen, with subscription terms and a consent checkbox shown before purchase.
    8. Risk reversal next to the money. Guarantee and cancel-anytime copy sit directly under the CTA.
    9. Post-purchase upsell. The confirmation screen offers a one-tap add for the next shipment.
    10. Test, do not assume. Validate each change with an A/B test against the current page. Lift varies by traffic and offer.
